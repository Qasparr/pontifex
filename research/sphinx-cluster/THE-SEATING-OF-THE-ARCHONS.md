## II. THE TEST, PRE-REGISTERED

*Stated before the results, and not moved after the throw.*

**THESIS T1 — "the exact matches are the Archons."** The battery's
exact fits, taken as MARKS, constitute the seating. Mark classes,
defined in advance, strongest first:

- **POSITION-mark** — fixes a vowel's triad AND its position within
  the triad. (Battery yield entering the test: Α only.)
- **TRIAD-mark** — fixes the triad only: two independent partitions
  (Frame A, the sketch's order; Frame B, the decade classes) agree
  on the triad while parting on position.
- **VALUE-mark** — a cross-tongue identical value, or a dress-price
  identity, corroborating the vowel's seat-value, where the battery
  flagged the identity as a finding. Two species, printed per mark:
  *dress-price* (a Latin dress carrying the vowel's own Greek price,
  where dresses generally lose it) and *mirror-bank* (a daughter
  bank carrying the letter at its Greek price entire).
- **PAIR-mark** — an exact pairing with the house's other nine.
- **STRUCTURE-mark** — an exact property of the whole set, belonging
  to no single seat.

The test: **(a) COVERAGE** — each of the nine seats is enumerated
and graded MARKED at the strongest class its marks support, or
UNMARKED. **(b) CONSISTENCY** — no two marks may assign the same
vowel conflicting seats. The Frame A / Frame B partings on Ε, Η, Ι,
Ω are *frame disagreements*, printed as such in the table; the test
rules on whether the exact-match marks *themselves* conflict.
**(c) CLASS DISCIPLINE** — the thesis passes for a seat exactly at
the class its marks earn: the seating may claim position only where
a POSITION-mark stands, triad only where a TRIAD-mark stands,
value-standing only where a VALUE-mark stands.

**PASS (whole)** = all nine seats marked at some class, with zero
conflicts among the marks. **PARTED** = some seats marked, some
unmarked, or a conflict at a named coordinate. **FAILS** = conflicts
at the core seats, or coverage near-empty.

**THESIS T2 — "the cross is the dial cross hairs for the inversion
of clock to counter."** Tested at three coordinates: **(a) THE
AXIS** — does the dial of record carry a fixed cross, or an
axis-pair? A single N–S axis is a LINE, not a cross; what stands is
printed exactly. **(b) THE INVERSION** — the tridecagram's {13/5}
traversal and its inverse are computed: the inverse must pass
through the same stations in exact reverse order for clock →
counter to stand as an exact operation on the engine of record.
**(c) NON-CONTRADICTION** — the thesis relocates the cross's office
from seating to reference; it must contradict no battery finding.
**PASS** = (a) a cross or axis-pair stands in the record, (b) the
inversion computes exactly, (c) no contradiction. PARTED and FAILS
graded likewise, coordinates printed.
