# THE ZERO POINTS OF THE MOTHER, THE FATHER, AND FLUIDITY
## A boundary survey under the Zero-Point Directive

**Ordered by Johnathan "Qasparr" Monroe, 2026-10-28:** "pin point the
actual Zero-point where it went from Matriarch to Patriarch and where
it went fluidity." Method per his standing directive: no verdict blob —
boundary coordinates per register, and the measured domain named where
agreement is total or absent.

## I. Matriarch → Patriarch: no single planetary zero point exists

The desk reports the negative result first because it is the finding:
**the evidence contains no one moment where "matriarchy" became
"patriarchy" worldwide.** What it contains is a boundary in each
register — and the registers do not share a date. The zero point of
the popular claim itself is a conflation: matrilineal descent,
matrilocal residence, and goddess worship are each attested widely;
none of them is female political rule, and no universal matriarchal
stage has been evidenced. Bachofen's *Mutterrecht* (1861) is the
concept's birthday, not the era's.

## II. The boundary coordinates, register by register

**Demographic (hardest evidence) — Europe, ~3000–2500 BCE.**
Ancient DNA: Corded Ware populations carry ~75% Yamnaya-related
steppe ancestry; steppe herders expanded westward around 3000 BC; male
lineages show 50–90% turnover while maternal lines comparatively
persist — a patrilineal, patrilocal package arriving as a datable
wave. Coordinates: (northern/central Europe, the steppe threshold,
measured in Y-chromosomes). A regional arrival, not a global origin.

**Mythic-textual — Babylon, the Enuma Elish (Tablet IV).**
Copies survive from the 7th century BCE; composition is placed in the
Bronze Age (Hammurabi/early Kassite era, ~18th–16th c. BCE), with some
scholars favoring c. 1100 BCE. In the text the primordial mother
Tiamat is slain by the young god Marduk and the ordered world is built
from her divided body — creation retold as the son's victory over the
mother, after which Marduk takes fifty names and the kingship of the
gods. The Hebrew *tehom* ("the Deep," Gen 1) is Tiamat's cognate
ghost: the mother survives in the successor text as a word.

**Juridical-dramatic — Athens, 458 BCE, the Oresteia.**
The most precise zero point in the entire inquiry, because the text
stages it as a measurement: the human jury splits EQUAL. Apollo
argues the redefinition — "The mother is no parent; it is the father
who is the parent, while the mother is merely the fosterer of an
implanted seed" — and Athena casts the single deciding vote for
Orestes. Engels' summary of Bachofen: "Father-right has triumphed
over mother-right." Boundary coordinates: (the Areopagus, 458 BCE,
margin = one divine vote). And the old order is not annihilated: the
Furies are re-seated within the city as the Eumenides — form changes,
function persists. His thesis's grammar clause, in civic dress.

## III. Fluidity: the record opens fluid; rigidity is the later build

The question's direction reverses under evidence. Gender fluidity is
not a late development in the record — it is present at the record's
opening, as an attribute of the goddess and an institution of her
temples:

- **Enheduanna of Ur (c. 2300 BCE), the first named author in
  history**, hymns Inanna: "To turn a man into a woman and a woman
  into a man are yours, Inanna."
- **The gala priests** are documented in temple personnel lists from
  **2600–2500 BCE**; men who became gala sometimes adopted female
  names and sang in *eme-sal*, the Sumerian literary dialect normally
  reserved for the speech of female characters.
- **The kurgarrū and assinnu**, servants of Ishtar in the Akkadian
  period, dressed in female clothing in her temples; an Akkadian hymn
  describes Ishtar as transforming men into women and women into men.

So the fluidity zero point sits at (Uruk/Ur, third millennium BCE,
inside the Mother's own house) — fluidity is archaic, institutional,
and hers. The modern vocabulary is a thin recent layer over it: the
term "transgender" itself was only coined in the 1960s.

**Inside his own engine**, the same boundary was already drawn: in the
Divine-Nine thesis, gender is a FORM operator (tāʾ marbūṭa — the
feminine ending that changes the letter's form and, by convention,
its decade) — gender moves form, never function. The gala evidence
is that clause wearing a temple: gender reassigned at the level of
dress, name, dialect, and office — the form — while the person, the
function, persists.
