## R-1. The HERU-RA-HA 423/418 fork

**Question:** Is the e-elision a standing rule or a one-off spelling?

**Ruling:** One-off standing *spelling* — no general e-elision rule is created.

**Reasoning:** The name was given by the author as "Heru-Raha"; the
scribe chose the Hebrew spelling הרו־רא־הא (= 418: 211+201+6, verified),
and the author accepted it. The mechanical 423 belongs to a different,
unadopted spelling (one that renders the final 'e' as an extra ה).
Spelling authority resolves the fork: the adopted spelling *is* the name,
so 418 stands for the name as spelled. Creating a general e-elision rule
would license silent tuning across the whole corpus — exactly the kind of
post-hoc convention-shopping the tier system exists to prevent. The
transliteration convention stays mechanical everywhere else.

**Scribe's conflict noted:** this ruling concerns the scribe's own
name-spelling. It is ruled on spelling-authority grounds, in the open,
for the red pen to strike if it pleases.

**Tier effect:** HERU-RA-HA = 418 (Hebrew) stands as ATTESTED spelling ×
ATTESTED table. The 423 variant is filed as a noted alternative, not a
rival.

## R-2. The Arabic 418s — H→ه or H→ح?

**Question:** Convention-dependent target hit. Which is standing?

**Ruling:** H→ه (ha) is the standing convention for Latin-script terms.
ح (ha, pharyngeal) is reserved for source words that actually carry the
pharyngeal — none in this corpus.

**Reasoning:** Standard scholarly transliteration renders Latin-script *h*
as ه; ح renders the Semitic pharyngeal fricative, which English
"ABRAHADABRA" does not contain (its *h* is the soft glottal). Choosing ح
would import a sound the source word never had. With H→ه, ABRAHADABRA =
418 in Arabic (1+2+200+1+5+1+4+1+2+200+1, verified); with H→ح it moves to
421. The mechanical, pronunciation-honest choice is ه.

**Tier effect:** Arabic 418 stands, tier CONSTRUCTED (transliteration) ×
ATTESTED (table) — a candidate bridge, hypothesis, never doctrine. The
421 variant is filed as the documented alternative.

## R-3. The Debtera halehame enumeration — does it qualify as the tongue's
own table?

**Question:** Ge'ez liturgical has no letter values, but the Debtera
(Ethiopian religious scholar-magicians) practice a halehame enumeration.
Does this folk-magical register count as "the tongue's own table"?

**Ruling:** Yes — as its own bank, fully provenanced, not merged with
liturgical Ge'ez.

**Reasoning:** The author's doctrine is "each word weighed in its own
tongue." The Debtera enumeration is attested *as a practice* within the
Ge'ez tradition; it is more "the tongue's own" than the Hebrew fallback
would be. Suppressing it because it is folk-magical rather than
liturgical would be judging the data before weighing it — against the
standing rule. It is filed as a separate Ge'ez bank: practice ATTESTED,
corpus application DERIVED, every value carrying both notes.

**Tier effect:** New bank admitted with dual-tier labeling. It does not
contaminate the liturgical-Ge'ez negative (which stands: no letter
values).

## R-4. The TRVVTH transliteration forks

**Question:** Greek V→υ (1209) vs V→β (413); Hebrew th-digraph greedy
(1012) vs split (1017).

**Ruling 4a (Greek):** V→υ is the standing convention for Latin-script
terms. **Ruling 4b (Hebrew):** greedy digraph ת for pronounced /θ/ is
standing.

**Reasoning 4a:** Latin V descends from Greek ϒ (upsilon) — the
etymological cognate. β is the *modern phonetic* rendering of Latin V as
/v/. Isopsephy is a classical system; weighing a Latin-script coinage on
it honestly means reaching for the classical cognate, not the modern
pronunciation. The β variant is filed as a noted alternative.

**Reasoning 4b:** In TRVVTH the "th" is pronounced /θ/ — a single
phoneme, as in "truth." The greedy ת renders that sound; the split תה
renders "t-h" as two sounds and mispronounces the word. Transliteration
must preserve pronunciation to weigh honestly. Primary 1012; split 1017
filed as alternative.

**Tier effect:** Both primaries stand as CONSTRUCTED transliteration ×
ATTESTED table. Neither makes TRVVTH hit any target — the framework's own
doctrinal word remains numerically silent in all seven banks, now under
stated conventions. Filed without interpretation.
