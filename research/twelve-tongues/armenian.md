## 2. Tier justification

**ATTESTED — the tradition's own table.** The alphabetic numeral system is the tradition's own (created with Mashtots' alphabet, c. 405 AD; Wikipedia). It was in live use in Armenian manuscript colophons for dating (MELA manual), i.e. the same kind of manuscript attestation that grounds the Greek and Hebrew banks. It is **not DERIVED from Greek**: Wikipedia states the *principles* are the same as the ancient Greek and Hebrew systems, but the letter mapping is independent (see §5). No part of the table is constructed by the scribe.

**Corrections to the task's premises** (TRVVTH: errors are named, never obeyed silently):
- The task brief stated "Ք=40 … Օ=800". This is wrong. The attested table has **Ք=9000** and **Օ=10000**. The brief appears to have scrambled the values; the scribe weighed with the sourced table, not the brief.
- The task brief implied Armenian values "parallel the Greek Milesian scheme". The scheme is an analogy, not a parallel table — the letter mapping is largely independent (see §5).
- **Caveat on Օ and Ֆ:** these two letters were added in the 13th century, after Arabic numerals were already in use; Wikipedia notes they "sometimes have a numerical value assigned to them" (10000 / 20000). They are recorded with that caveat. Neither appears in the corpus transliterations, so the caveat has no effect on the weighings.

**Term weighings carry a compound tier: ATTESTED table × CONSTRUCTED transliteration map.** The values are the tradition's; the path from English to Armenian letters is the scribe's proposal. In the CSV the tier column reads "ATTESTED-table/CONSTRUCTED-map".

## 5. The Greek-parallel analysis (the §4 question, answered plainly)

The Armenian system does **not** parallel the Greek Milesian table letter-for-letter; it shares the *principle* (ciphered-additive alphabetic numerals — same principle as Greek and Hebrew, per Wikipedia) while assigning its own letters to the values. Where the two banks meet, it is family resemblance, not the same instrument:

| band | Greek | Armenian |
|---|---|---|
| 6–9 | ϛ=6, ζ=7, η=8, θ=9 (digamma slot occupied) | Զ=6, Է=7, Ը=8, Թ=9 (**no digamma** — the 6–9 band sits one letter earlier) |
| 10–90 | ι=10 … ϟ=90 (koppa) | Ժ=10 … Ղ=90 (no koppa — ordinary letter) |
| 100 | ρ=100 | Ճ=100 (aligns by position, different letter) |
| 200–900 | σ=200, τ=300, υ=400, φ=500, χ=600, ψ=700, ω=800, ϡ=900 | Մ=200, Յ=300, Ն=400, Շ=500, Ո=600, Չ=700, Պ=800, Ջ=900 — **independent letters throughout; no sampi analog** |
| 1000–9000 | ͵α–͵θ with keraia multiplier | dedicated letters Ռ–Ք (different mechanism) |
| 10000+ | ͵ι etc. | Օ=10000, Ֆ=20000 (late additions) |

So: the Armenian numerals are an **independent invention on the same architectural principle**, not a derivative of the Milesian table. Two consequences for the parent synthesis:
1. A convergent word-value shared by the Greek and Armenian banks is **partially independent evidence** — it reflects the shared ciphered-additive architecture (alphabet order × decimal bands), not a copied table. The independence is real at the letter level (different letters at 400–900, no epissemones) but not total at the structural level (both inherit the Phoenician-ordered alphabet with decimal ciphering). Weight it as family evidence, not as two witnesses to one text.
2. Any bridge that lands on **both** Greek and Armenian values is structurally *stronger* than one landing on Greek alone — the two tables agree on the number through different letter paths. (This bank offers no such case in the primary convention — a negative result worth one line.)

## 6. Collisions, mergers, losses

- **Internal collisions:** none — all eleven values are distinct.
- **Mergers fired:** C/K→կ (CELL); TH→թ (TRVVTH, THELEMA); X→խ collapsing /ks/ (AXONEME); U→ու two-letter digraph (HERU-RA-HA); V→վ for the Latin V-that-is-U (TRVVTH).
- **Information loss:** the C/K merger and the X→խ collapse erase distinctions the Latin originals carried; the O→ո choice deliberately refuses the 10000-valued օ (documented alternative); the ու digraph makes U the heaviest sound in the corpus (HERU-RA-HA is dominated by ր=5000 ×2 and ւ=7000). Every loss is named here and in the CSV.
- **The ր=5000 / վ=3000 / տ=4000 cluster** dominates several weighings (TRVVTH, HERU-RA-HA, ABRAHADABRA, QIRA, LIBERTY all exceed 9000) — an artifact of where R, V, T fall in the Mashtots order, not a finding.

## 7. Negative results (given their full weight)

1. **No target-number hits** in eleven terms, primary convention or any alternative.
2. **No established bridge crosses**: CELL=32→125; AXONEME=77→1251; OZ=77→606; THELEMA=93→250; AGAPE=93→810; ABRAHADABRA=418→10083.
3. **Greek values do not transfer**: the Greek bank's THELEMA=93 and ABRAHADABRA=418 produce different numbers here, because the letter mappings are independent (§5). The 93 and 418 bridges are Greek-bank (and Hebrew-bank) phenomena on this evidence.
4. **The task's stated values were wrong** (Ք≠40, Օ≠800) and the "parallel scheme" claim was overstated — both corrected above rather than obeyed.

## 8. Limits and open questions for the red pen

- The transliteration map is the scribe's construction; a native-speaker philological review (classical Armenian orthography of loanwords) could revise several calls (especially TH→թ vs տ+հ, and the U/V/W cluster).
- The օ/Ֆ "sometimes assigned" caveat (Wikipedia) means the late values are weaker than the rest of the table — flagged, unused in the corpus.
- Chance-convergence warning (METHOD §5) applies in full: with Armenian's large values (R=5000 etc.), big-number coincidences are cheap; none claimed.
