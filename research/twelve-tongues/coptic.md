### 1.2 Tier justification — ATTESTED (with descent documented)

The task asked me to decide: ATTESTED (living manuscript tradition) or
DERIVED (via Greek descent)? My ruling: **ATTESTED, with the Greek descent
stated plainly and never hidden.**

For ATTESTED: Coptic scribes genuinely wrote numbers with these letters —
the overline-marked alphabetic numeral system is described as a fact of
the script by Wikipedia and by the Institute of Coptic Studies (Dr. Isaac:
"most Greek letters were also used as numerals"), and the Epact digit
block's existence is manuscript evidence of the letter-numeral practice
it superseded. This is the tradition's own table, used in the
tradition's own manuscripts.

Against pure-DERIVED: calling it merely "Greek via descent" would hide the
Coptic-specific content — the numeral-only ⲋ (6), ϥ taking the koppa
90-slot as a native letter, the Coptic sampi Ⳁ for 900, and the five
Demotic letters that the attested system leaves valueless. Those are
Coptic facts, not Greek ones. So the honest tier is ATTESTED (the
tradition's own table) **with** the documented descent noted in
`table_used` on every row.

### 1.3 Transliteration convention — COPT-TRANS-v1 (CONSTRUCTED)

Mechanical Latin→Coptic, greedy digraphs first: TH→ⲑ, SH→ϣ, CH→ⲭ.
Letter map: A→ⲁ B→ⲃ C→ⲕ D→ⲇ E→ⲉ F→ϥ G→ⲅ H→ϩ I→ⲓ J→ϫ K→ⲕ L→ⲗ M→ⲙ N→ⲛ
O→ⲟ P→ⲡ Q→ⲕ R→ⲣ S→ⲥ T→ⲧ U→ⲟⲩ V→ⲃ W→ⲟⲩ X→ⲝ Y→ⲓ Z→ⲍ. Hyphens dropped
(separators, not letters — not zeroed). Case: Coptic had no original
case distinction (Wikipedia), so case is normalization-free.

Every choice stated: X→ⲝ because Coptic has the dedicated /ks/ letter
(ⲝ is attested "/ks/ (only in Greek loanwords)" — our terms are all
foreign, so the loanword reading is the honest one; the X→ⲭ variant is
weighed and labelled as anti-hit). V→ⲃ because Latin V is the consonant
b/v and ⲃ spans b~v~w across dialects (the vowel reading V→ⲟⲩ is weighed
as variant). F→ϥ and H→ϩ because Coptic has dedicated Demotic letters —
with the stated consequence that ϩ is valueless, so H-bearing terms fail
closed. TH→ⲑ (not ⲧϩ) because the dedicated letter exists — "where Coptic
has a dedicated letter, prefer it," per the task. Y→ⲓ (i-sound; Y→ⲩ
weighed as variant). Q→ⲕ (no q letter; merges with C and K).

### 1.4 Mergers and information loss (what the weighing destroys)

- **C/K/Q → ⲕ** — three-way merger (fires in QIRA, CELL).
- **B/V → ⲃ** — V's vowel life (/u/) is lost in the primary reading.
- **U/W → ⲟⲩ** — the digraph is weighed as two letters (ⲟ=70 + ⲩ=400),
  which is where TRVVTH's 1349 variant comes from.
- **I/Y → ⲓ** — merger (fires in LIBERTY).
- **E → ⲉ** — epsilon/eta merged at transliteration time (THELEMA,
  AGAPE); the authentic Greek spellings (ⲏ) are weighed as labelled
  variants so the loss is visible, not hidden.
- **H → ϩ — total loss**: ϩ has no numeral value, so HERU-RA-HA and
  ABRAHADABRA fail closed to UNKNOWN. This is the single largest
  information-loss event in the bank.
- **J → ϫ** — dedicated letter, no loss, valueless slot not fired by
  any corpus term (no J in the corpus).

### 1.7 The Greek-descent analysis (the anti-finding, stated plainly)

Where Coptic and Greek agree, **the agreement is almost entirely shared
Milesian descent, not independent evidence.** The Coptic alphabetic
numeral system is explicitly "based on Greek numerals" (Wikipedia); the
24 Greek-origin letters hold identical values to isopsephy by
inheritance. So:

- OZ=77 (Coptic) agreeing with OZ=77 (Greek) is the *same data in a
  Coptic typeface* — continuity, not corroboration. It says "the bridge
  AXONEME=77=עז survives translation into Coptic," which is real and
  worth recording, but it does not double the evidence.
- AXONEME=231, BABALON=156, QIRA=131, CELL=85, LIBERTY=457/847 agreeing
  across the two banks are likewise single-sourced (Milesian) values.
- The genuinely *Coptic* content — the part that is not just Greek with
  different glyphs — is: ⲋ=6 as a numeral-only letter, ϥ=90 replacing
  koppa as a native letter, Ⳁ=900 as the Coptic sampi, the five
  valueless Demotic letters, and the manuscript practice itself
  (overline marking; the Epact digits that superseded letter-numerals
  in Bohairic). The negative results are also genuinely Coptic:
  HERU-RA-HA and ABRAHADABRA fail closed because Coptic has a dedicated
  /h/ letter (ϩ) with no numeral value — Greek, lacking a consonantal
  H, could still weigh them (623 / 221). The Coptic bank is *stricter*
  than the Greek bank here, and the strictness is informative: it shows
  where the Milesian system simply does not reach.
- One real divergence: **TRVVTH**. The Greek worker's primary
  (V→upsilon) gives 1209; the Coptic primary (V→ⲃ) gives **413**; my
  V→ⲟⲩ variant gives 1349. All three are convention-dependent readings
  of the Roman V — none is "the" value. Flagged for the red pen.
