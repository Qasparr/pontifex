## How to read this

- **Cell** — COLUMN/row, with the column's title and the row's meaning for context.
- **Proposal** — the candidate filling. Every proposal carries our flag:
  `PONTIFEX-proposed 2026-10-13`.
- **Tier** — `attested` (the word exists in that tongue with that value) /
  `derived` (computed by rule from attested material) / `constructed` (our
  construction — hypothesis, never doctrine).
- **Bridge** — `yes` only when TWO honest banks agree on the same word.
  Transliteration agreements and chance coincidences are named as such,
  never counted as bridges.
- **INTENTIONALLY EMPTY (certified)** — the cell was examined and left blank
  deliberately, with the reasoning recorded. An empty cell is a finding too.
- Machine ledger: `hidden_files/fillings.csv` (590 weighing rows).
- Worker thought logs: `hidden_files/worker-NN-log.md` (all 24, ultra-verbose).
