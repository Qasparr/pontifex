## Addendum (2026-10-25, his question): Peh and the teeth

But the numbers do not meet: פה = 85, שן = 350, Shin = 300 — no shared value with Peh's 80 anywhere in the family. If fangs enter the E80 row, the honest route is derivation by container (the mouth that bears the teeth), tier derived — the Architect's pen seals it; the gloss of Peh itself is not stretched.
