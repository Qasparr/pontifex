The founder ruled, 2026-11-15 10:54 EST, verbatim: **"Then WELLZ are Powers."** The five standing citation wells of 2026-09-28 — **Law, Scripture, Black's, Hip-hop, Fiction & film** — are Powers, not passive sources. This annex decides nothing. Its commission is narrower and harder: for each of the five Wells and each of the Four Powers of the Sphinx — **Scire** (to know), **Audere** (to dare), **Velle** (to will), **Tacere** (to keep silence) — lay out **what evidence exists**: the weighings in Greek expressions (the proof seat, per his ruling of the same hour: *"Then proves in Greek or thvs invalid"*), the Latin witnesses beside them, and the functional and textual testimony each well can honestly give for each power. Reasoning stands inline with every correspondence, per the standing rule for his tables. Tiers are marked on every claim.

**Where a cell has no honest evidence, the cell is printed EMPTY. An unseated cell is a finding, not a failure** — under his maxim of the same hour, *"The full inquiry verifies judgement"*: the inquiry that returns an empty cell has still verified something, namely that the seat is not proven there. A force-fit cell would be a false witness, and this tribunal strikes false witnesses by leaving their seats empty in print.

### III.4 What the numbers actually say — and what they do not

1. **At the level of exact value, the numeric evidence seats NO cell.** No well's Greek face equals any power's Greek face (430 · 612 · 245 · 719 against 1263 · 441 · 462 · 221). No well's English name equals any power's Latin name in ordinal or in Agrippa (36/21 · 129/627 · 48/126 · 72/195 · 76/217 against 54/187 · 54/295 · 56/250 · 52/194). This is printed as the annex's first finding: **if any well is ever seated on any power, the seating will not be by number.** The proof bank, asked honestly, returns twenty empty numeric cells.
2. **Two genus-level witnesses, printed as measurements and claimed as nothing past them.** (a) **WELL ordinal 52 = TACERE ordinal 52** — the genus word *well* shares the ordinal seat of *Tacere* in the witness bank. Reasoning for printing it: it is exact, in a named bank, and it touches the genus, not any individual well; it is evidence about the word WELL, not about Fiction, Law, or any other member. (b) **HIPHOP Agrippa 195 = POWER Agrippa 195** — the one well whose name meets the genus word POWER in a witness bank. Reasoning: exact and named; but it meets *power*, not any one of the four Powers, so it cannot prefer Audere over Scire or any other seat. (Filed beside it, from the essay §V: WELLS Agrippa 135 = SIGIL Agrippa 135 — the wells and the mark, genus to genus, same fence.)
3. **The powers among themselves** (printed so the pen sees the whole board): SCIRE ordinal 54 = AUDERE ordinal 54 — Know and Dare share a witness seat and are separated by Agrippa (187 / 295) and by Greek (1263 / 441); VELLE ordinal 56 = WILL ordinal 56, the house's standing seat. These are relations inside the Powers, not evidence for any well's seat.
4. **The reduction register — fenced, weakest tier, NOT seat evidence.** Reductions coincide where exact values do not: γραφή 612 → 9 and τόλμα 441 → 9 share the reduction 9, as does πηγή 99 → 9; γνῶσις, θέλησις, θέλημα, and δύναμις all reduce to 3. A reduction is a one-in-nine meeting by construction; this annex prints these coincidences for completeness of the record and **claims nothing from them**. Any future argument that seats Scripture on Audere because "612 and 441 both come to 9" is answered in advance, here: the reduction register is not a proof, and under the founder's Greek gate a reduction is not a Greek expression of the claim.
