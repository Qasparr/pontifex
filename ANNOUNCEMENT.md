Most gematria is weighed in one tongue and quoted in another. That is not a bridge. That is a wish.

A bridge needs two honest banks. PONTIFEX is the instrument that weighs each word in its own tongue — Hebrew by mispar hechrechi, Greek by isopsephy, Latin by its own ordinal — and then asks the only question that matters: **does the number match?** When it matches, the match is the bridge. Two tongues, one number.

The canonical span: **AXONEME = 77** in English ordinal; **עז = 77** in Hebrew. Two banks, one river. That is the method. That is the purpose.

What ships in the Third Span:

- **The weighing engine** — attested tables for Hebrew, Greek, Latin ordinal, Agrippa Latin, and Arabic abjad. No table, no weighing. No weighing, no bridge.
- **The Hebrew fallback** — for tongues with no table of their own, the tradition's own move: transliterate into Hebrew and weigh there. Lossy where scripts exceed 22 letters, and every merger is documented on the receipt.
- **Braille as carrier** — 64 dot-patterns swallow most alphabets whole, lossless where Hebrew would merge. Values 1–64 in canonical order: my construction, stated as such, never paraded as discovery.
- **The music bridge** — note names in the English letter tradition *are* Latin letters, so a melody is a word spelled in notes. C–E–G = 15 = O. Constructed extraction, stated conventions, flagged hypothesis — the red pen decides, not the machine.
- **Three-tier provenance on everything** — ATTESTED / DERIVED / CONSTRUCTED. Convergences on attested tables are candidate bridges; convergences touching the constructed tier are hypotheses. The engine never lets a hypothesis wear doctrine's clothes.
- **The full API** — an importable Python package, a script-ready CLI with a hard exit-code contract (0 = converged, 3 = diverged, 1 = error), and an HTTP JSON API that binds loopback only, because bridges face inward until their banks are proven. The instrument checks itself first: `selftest` is green before anything else runs.

The name is Latin for *bridge-builder* — approved by the red pen. ("Oracle" was declined: there is already an Oracle, and it sells databases.)

The emblem: a golden arch with **77 on the keystone**, Hebrew אבג on one bank, Latin ABC on the other, a compass rose behind it. You cross from either side.

Three spans are built. The atlas — every script weighed, every bridge charted, the full correspondence map — is the work ahead.
